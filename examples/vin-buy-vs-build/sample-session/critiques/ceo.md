# CEO critique: red-teaming the hybrid consensus

## Steelman: buy
Buy is my mandate's best rejected option. Value in month 2 hits revenue_q1 harder than anything else. It frees 20 of 24 team-weeks for other revenue work and leads the case by about 259k over three years. Price the S-040 remap at the CTO's 8 to 10 weeks (72k to 90k) and buy still looks ahead on paper. Keep the marketplace out of the vendor's ID space and buy wins EBITDA.

## Attack: hybrid's 580,744 is a number nobody has funded
Hybrid's 12 team-weeks buy a thin layer, the vendor adapter and the S-009 migration. Read the plans: CEO, CTO, CPO and CDO all push own decoding to a later go/no-go (week 26 or month 9). Yet business_case.yaml assumes vendor_share 0.55 in year 2 and 0.35 in year 3, so fees shrink. That assumes a home-market decoder (build's 22 weeks, minus the layer) that no position plans and no candidate costs. On top of that, hybrid carries 9,000 a month run cost from month 1 when the "partial data licences" only start in year 2, and Year 3 net equals Year 2 even though vendor_share falls. If decoding never ships, hybrid is buy plus a layer, with run cost six times higher and fees growing 15% a year. That is my margin requirement broken quietly.

## Proposal
Split it. Re-run the case for **hybrid-thin** with vendor_share [1.0, 1.0, 1.0] and score that number. Make own home-market decoding a separate candidate with its own effort, licences and owner, decided at month 9 on measured fees. Sign the vendor with a 2-year price cap.
