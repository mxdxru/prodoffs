# CPO critique: red-teaming the hybrid consensus

## Steelman: buy
On my mandate, buy is the strongest option we rejected. It takes 4 team-weeks, delivers value in month 2 and puts VIN entry live in January, well clear of the spring season. Customers get the same 0.95 hit rate and 0.985 precision as with hybrid. It also leaves 20 weeks for workshop UX: scan, ambiguity question, dropdown prefill. Every role conceded that buy keyed to our own IDs would be acceptable. That means the gap between buy and hybrid is a sequencing question, not a reason to choose hybrid over buy.

## Attack: five hybrids, one number
"Hybrid" hides five different plans. The CTO puts VIN entry at weeks 10-12, after the search, cart and returns migration. That means March at best, so it misses season_window. I put VIN entry at weeks 3-6. The CMO goes public at weeks 13-14. The 12 weeks are the same in every plan, but the order of work inside them differs.

The money doesn't add up either. The 12 weeks pay for the layer and the S-009 migration. They do not pay for own decoding, as the CTO admits. Even so, the case books vendor_share at 0.55 in year 2 and 0.35 in year 3, and year 3 still nets exactly what year 2 does (CEO). So part of the 580,744 rests on work nobody has funded, and part of it doesn't even show up in the numbers.

## Proposal
Split hybrid into two parts:
- **H1 (about 8 weeks):** a thin mapping table plus VIN entry through the vendor, with a gate that VIN is live for workshops by 1 March.
- **H2:** the migration and own decoding, as a separate candidate with its own effort.

Re-run the case with vendor_share at 1.0 until H2 is approved.
