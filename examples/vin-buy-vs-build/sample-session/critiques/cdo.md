# CDO critique: red-teaming the hybrid consensus

## Steelman: build
Only build makes the decoding ours, not just the ID. Every rule can be audited and marketplace sellers get a fully owned identity. That is data_ownership, 0.45 of my weight. Build is also the only version whose year-2 economics are funded: its 22 weeks pay for the decoder that hybrid only promises.

## Attack: hybrid's case prices work nobody funded
- All plans spend the 12 team-weeks on layer, vendor adapter and S-009 migration. But vendor_share [1.0, 0.55, 0.35] assumes an own home-market decoder from year 2, and neither the candidate nor any plan pays for it. CTO and CEO both flag this. So the 580,744 counts savings from the build we rejected.
- Year 3 equals Year 2 even though vendor_share falls from 0.55 to 0.35. Either the curve or run_cost is wrong.
- The 12 weeks are also double-booked. CPO launches VIN at week 10 and moves returns migration past the season, and CMO adds landing pages. My instrumentation and holdout (weeks 6-9) are in no other plan. If those weeks get cut, red line `measurable` breaks and fitment_return_reduction 0.60 stays a guess, as in D-0016.

## Proposal
Split hybrid in two. **hybrid-layer**: 12 weeks, vendor_share [1.0, 1.0, 1.0], instrumentation and a 10 per cent holdout in done_when. **own-decoding**: a separate candidate with its own effort and licences, gated on 30 days of shadow-decode precision of at least 0.985. Rerun `prodoffs.py case` on those inputs.
