# Round 1: Data

**Challenge to the strongest option (hybrid).** The run-cost fix is weaker than the positions say. At the reply's 0.09 fee the breach is about 2,800 a month (our arithmetic), and fees are about 8,775. Cache alone must avoid about 32% of paid lookups to close it. Executive's "11% off" was calculated at 0.08; at 0.09 it is about 16%. Volume tiers are the lever that closes it. No repeat-VIN share is in the files, so the cache saving is unknown.

**Weakest assumptions.**
- Legal: the veto on buy and hybrid is now a signing condition (signed DPA), not a dead end. Liability cap, audit and breach notice are still open.
- Product: the -33% returns target cannot be read by the owner's mid-May date. A mid-March launch with a 30 to 45 day return lag gives about four weeks of matured returns.
- Marketing: "0.08 per VIN session" is per lookup, not per session.

**Amendment with receipts.** The reply allows caching for our customers and limits VIN retention to 30 days, so my storage precondition is partly met. Still open: a licence to keep mapped results after termination, and bulk export on exit. Without them the asset does not compound and S-040 returns at exit.

**Conditions.**
1. Pre-check before signature: 2,000 VINs from past orders, stratified by country and model year, about 180 at 0.09. At 1,000 VINs the 95% interval around 98% is about ±0.9 pt, too close to a 97% floor. Ground truth comes from a catalogue-expert sample, not self-reported returns.
2. Holdout: 10% without VIN entry for 8 weeks, as the owner answered. Keep the 5% control running until cohorts entering through April have matured. That is June, not mid-May. Pooled, the control yields about 170 fitment returns over three months.
3. Decision rule fixed before launch: scale only if precision is at least 97% and conversion lift is at least about +0.6 pt. The 3.8% vs 4.8% gap includes self-selection, so only the holdout gives a causal read.

**Final stance:** Hybrid, phased, only with the 2,000-VIN pre-check, the 5% control and a post-termination data licence.
