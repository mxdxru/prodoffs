# Analytics snapshot (last full month)

Fictional numbers for this example; they match `business_case.yaml`.

- Part-search sessions: about 600,000 a month.
- Sessions where the vehicle is known (dropdown selection): 35%.
- Search-to-order conversion: 4.8% when the vehicle is known, 3.8% when it is not.
- Average order value 118 EUR, gross margin 28%.
- Return rate 17% of orders; 45% of returns are tagged "does not fit".
- In a survey on the search page, 41% of customers without a selected vehicle said they would enter a VIN if offered; the business case assumes 25% actually will.
- Customers can save vehicles in a "my garage" profile today (make, model, engine); planned: store the VIN there for repeat orders.
