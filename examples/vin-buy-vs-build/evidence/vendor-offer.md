# Vendor offer: VIN decoding API (summary of the commercial proposal)

Fictional vendor, anonymised for this example.

- **Price:** 0.08 EUR per lookup, monthly minimum 6,000 EUR.
- **Price review:** annual, "up to 15% per year in line with data acquisition costs".
- **Term:** 36 months; termination for convenience after month 12 with 6 months' notice.
- **Coverage and quality (vendor's claim):** 95% of European VINs resolved; 98.5% precision on the vendor's own test set.
- **Identifiers:** results are returned as the vendor's proprietary vehicle IDs, with optional mapping to two common catalog standards at extra cost.
- **Data:** "VINs submitted through the API may be retained for up to 24 months and used to improve the service." Hosting in the EU; sub-processors "available on request".
- **SLA:** 99.5% monthly availability; service credits capped at 10% of the monthly fee.
